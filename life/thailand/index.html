Bangkok streets, temples, hostels, Phuket nights — and a Muay Thai session that ended up becoming the part of the trip I’ll remember most.

Bangkok → Phuket · 2026

A vacation, until the gloves came out.

Thailand had been on my list for the beaches, food, temples, and the energy of Bangkok, but the trip became more personal once Muay Thai was part of it. I went from rainy streets and crowded lanes in Bangkok to temple grounds, Chinatown at night, hostel stays, and then south to Phuket — seeing a version of Thailand that felt equal parts chaotic, beautiful, and completely alive.

The part I’ll remember most was training at Tiger Muay Thai in Phuket. I train regularly back home, but getting to put on gloves and work with a coach in Thailand made the whole trip feel different. The heat, the gym, the pace, and the fact that I was practicing something I care about in the country where it comes from turned a vacation into something I actually felt connected to.
