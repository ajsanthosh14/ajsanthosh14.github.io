A hiking trip that was too big to leave as a few photos — so I cut the memory into a film instead.

Grand Canyon · hiking · edited by me

The trail looked different once I got home.

Some trips stay in your head as individual moments. The Grand Canyon felt more like a sequence — the scale of the walls, long stretches of trail, tired legs, and those pauses where you look up and realize how far you still have to go.

After the trip, I put the footage together myself. Editing it became another way of going back through the hike: the parts that felt hard in the moment, the small scenes I almost forgot, and the views that made the whole thing worth carrying a camera for.
